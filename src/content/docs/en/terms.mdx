**algo** is a free site for learning algorithms and data structures. By using it, you agree to the terms below.

## What the site does with your code

- Everything you write in the exercises runs **in your browser**, in an isolated Web Worker with a time limit. Nothing is sent to a server.
- Each exercise's draft is saved in your browser's `localStorage`, so you don't lose your work. To erase it, clear the site's data in your browser.

## Privacy

- There are no accounts, no logins, no tracking cookies and no analytics.
- Fonts are served by Google Fonts and Fontshare. When the page loads, your browser requests them from those services, which receive your IP address, as on any site that uses their fonts.
- The hosting provider may record technical access data (IP, date, requested page) in server logs, for security and operation.
