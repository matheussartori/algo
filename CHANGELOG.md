## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-12

### Added

- Search topic: linear and binary search with a step-by-step player, Predict mode, exercises and review questions, in pt-BR and English.
- In-browser exercise runner (TypeScript or JavaScript, Web Worker, timeout).
- Landing page with a WebGPU particle background, GSAP and Lenis.
- Brand: mark, favicons, web manifest, OpenGraph images and a rendering script.
- SEO: sitemap, canonical URLs, hreflang, OpenGraph and Twitter cards, JSON-LD.
